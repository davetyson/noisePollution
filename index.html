🎸 Howdy Rock 'n' Rollers!

Welcome to Noise Pollution Rock School, where it's all about getting kids excited about making music—not bogging them down with boring formalities. Because in Rock ‘n’ Roll… there are no rules!

🎶 About the Program

Ages: 7–18

Class Length: 45 minutes

Schedule: Monday–Thursday at 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM

Location: My basement studio on Hamilton Mountain

No Experience? No Instrument? No problem—we supply everything!

At Noise Pollution, we believe music is a powerful tool for self-discovery and creative expression. So let’s learn, grow, make friends, and most importantly…

Let’s Rock!

- Matt Ellis

Classes

  • 🧑‍🎤 Class Details

    • Small group sessions: 2–5 kids per class

    • Instruments covered: Guitar, Bass, Drums, and Vocals

    • Music style: Rock and Pop jams, plus writing original songs

    • Kids learn a mix of covers of songs they love and create original music together

    • $300 per child for each 12-week term

    • Accommodations can be made for kids joining mid-term (just reach out)

    🎤 The Rock School Experience

    Every student enrolled in regular classes gets to participate in:

    • Our Christmas Concert – Celebrate the season with a fun, festive show

    • The Year-End Rock Show (June) – A high-energy performance that feels like a kids' rock festival

    These events are the highlight of each term, where students get to perform live, showcase their progress, and feel like real rock stars!

    🎧 Optional Add-On: Recording Session

    At the end of the year, older students have the exciting opportunity to take part in a professional-style recording session.

    • Students can record an original song they've written during class

    • Songs are uploaded to a website so band members can share and download their track

    • This experience is offered as an optional add-on and comes with an additional cost

⚡ Additional Services

• Birthday Jam Parties – A one-of-a-kind rock band experience for your child's special day

• Live Performances for Events & Festivals – Hire me to bring the noise to your next community event or festival

👉 Inquire for pricing - each event is custom and can be tailored to your needs
